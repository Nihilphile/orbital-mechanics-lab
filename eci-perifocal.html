The green ellipse and dashed target have the same size and shape. Initially, the ellipse lies in the equatorial plane with periapsis along X. After three rotations, the two outlines coincide.

At i = 0° or 180°, the node line is undefined. Here, Ω and ω follow a chosen convention; they cannot be determined independently from the orbit.

Show matrix and sign conventions

R denotes a right-handed active rotation matrix; vectors are columns. The columns of B are the P, Q, and W directions expressed in ECI. The animation rotates about successive local axes by Ω, i, and ω. Coordinate conversion uses the inverse: Bᵀ = Rz(−ω) Rx(−i) Rz(−Ω). The target ECI → PQW matrix is:

P points toward periapsis. Q is perpendicular to P in the orbital plane, in the direction of increasing true anomaly, and W = P × Q. This animation demonstrates a change of basis, not satellite motion along the orbit.
